# Pioneer — Steam Store Description

## Short Description (30 words)

Navigate 29 ports, trade eight commodities, and build a merchant fleet in a living ocean economy that shifts with every deal you make.

---

## Full Description

**Pioneer** is a single-player ocean trade and fleet management simulation set across a world of 29 ports spanning multiple regions. You begin with a single vessel and a modest stake of coin, and everything that follows is the result of decisions you make on the open water: which goods to buy, which ports to sail for, which routes to run when the market turns against you. The economy is not a backdrop — it is the opponent. Prices move on a three-minute micro-cycle of small fluctuations, respond in real time to your own buying and selling, and lurch in unpredictable directions when major market events strike once an hour. Reading price history across twenty data points is not optional; it is the skill that separates a prosperous merchant from one left anchored in debt.

Fleet management is where short-term thinking meets long-term ambition. Each crew member carries individual stats that affect the performance of the ship they sail on, and upgrading your fleet is a layered resource challenge — you balance cargo capacity against speed, operating costs against profit margins, and the temptation to expand against the discipline of consolidating what you have. Pioneer rewards players who plan three voyages ahead rather than three minutes ahead, while keeping every individual trade decision legible within thirty seconds of opening the port screen.

Built with React and Vite for smooth browser-native performance, Pioneer delivers a full-featured trading simulation without the friction of complex installation or heavy hardware demands. The dynamic market model — developed with a layered architecture that keeps micro-fluctuations, player-impact pricing, and macro market events fully independent — ensures that no two campaigns unfold the same way. Whether you are optimizing a single high-margin route or rebuilding after a catastrophic market event wiped out your reserves, every session in Pioneer has a story.

## Key Features

- **Living Ocean Economy** — Three independent pricing layers (micro-fluctuations, player impact, hourly market events) keep the market unpredictable and reactive across your entire playthrough.
- **29 Ports, 8 Commodities** — A wide world of trading opportunities with distinct regional price basins and risk levels per port.
- **Fleet & Crew Management** — Hire and develop crew members with individual stats; grow from a single ship to a multi-vessel merchant operation.
- **Price History Intelligence** — Track the last 20 price data points per commodity. Information is your most valuable cargo.
- **Meaningful Route Decisions** — Every route choice has consequences; market impact from your trades lingers and shapes the next window of opportunity.
- **Accessible Strategy Depth** — A clean HUD and streamlined port interface put the focus on decisions, not tutorials. First voyage insights within 30 seconds of docking.
