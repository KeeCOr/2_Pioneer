# Pioneer — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-09-05
**Developer:** Stoicent
**Game:** Pioneer (v1.8.2)
**Contact:** koheag2@stoicent.com

---

## Overview

Pioneer is a singleplayer, offline game. It does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with respect to user information.

---

## Data We Do Not Collect

Pioneer does **not** collect any of the following:

- Personal identifiers (name, email address, account credentials)
- Device identifiers (device ID, advertising ID, IP address)
- Location data (precise or approximate)
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Gameplay statistics or session data sent to external systems
- Crash reports transmitted to external servers
- Any data that could be used to identify or track you

---

## No Network Activity

Pioneer operates entirely offline. The game:

- Does not connect to the internet during gameplay
- Does not connect to any external server, analytics service, or third-party API
- Does not transmit data of any kind to the developer or any third party

> **Note on game architecture:** Pioneer is built with React and Vite and runs as a local browser-native application. The dynamic market economy (price fluctuations, fleet data, trade history) is computed entirely client-side. No data is sent outbound.

---

## Local Save Data

Pioneer stores game state locally on your device (fleet data, trade history, port progress, settings). This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not accessible to the developer
- Can be deleted at any time by clearing the game's local storage or removing the application directory

---

## No In-App Purchases

Pioneer does not offer in-app purchases, premium currency, loot boxes, or any monetization system that involves user financial data.

---

## No Third-Party SDKs or Advertising

Pioneer does not include:

- Advertising networks or ad SDKs
- Analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics)
- Social media SDKs or share integrations
- Any third-party library that accesses or transmits user data

---

## Children's Privacy

Because Pioneer collects no data of any kind, it is safe for players of all ages and complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and applicable regional children's privacy regulations.

---

## Steam Platform

If you purchase Pioneer through Steam, Valve Corporation's privacy policy applies to your Steam account and transaction. That policy is separate from this one and governs how Valve handles your data. Stoicent receives no personal data from Steam.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If Pioneer is ever updated to collect data (for example, optional cloud saves or leaderboards), this policy will be updated and a new effective date will be published before that version ships.
